Score = points on the line − deduction × miss, never below the minimum, where the miss is how far the wheel stopped short of the edge of the line: the line is painted with a width, and touching down anywhere on it earns everything. A width of 0 makes the line infinitely thin. Short and long have their own deduction because a landing short of the line is usually punished harder than one beyond it. Take-offs, fly-throughs and rejected events earn nothing.
